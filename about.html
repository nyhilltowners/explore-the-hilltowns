About this atlas

Hilltowns Folk Atlas

A living atlas of the NY Helderberg-Hudson Hilltowns — folklore and legends gathered from historical sources, alongside the people making and telling stories today. Every tale lists its original teller and source, stories are only as reliable as their tellers.

Additions, suggestions, corrections, complaints about the universe? Message us on Instagram at @NYHilltowners
