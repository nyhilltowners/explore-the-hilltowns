# Hilltowns POI, Deep-Time & Folklore Notes

Companion to the **Regional Climate Event Register**. ## Ice-age landform POIs

- **"A Bump in the Road" — County Rte 67, Freehold (Greene Co).** Across from the Freehold Airport, ~1 mi W of the Freehold four corners. A dangerous road hump + blind curve that the Tituses classify as a **geohazard** — but the "hump" is an **ice-age alluvial fan**: a relict canyon just uphill dumped meltwater sediment onto the valley flat as the glaciers left (~12,000+ yrs ago), building a fan the modern road rides over. Real accidents here — a helicopter evacuation ~2002. **Several more fans along the same Catskill Creek stretch** (one across from the airport "art gallery" building; another by Storey's Nursery, where the old gray farmhouse sits on it). The O'Hara Corners hill apparently held ice after the rest of the valley cleared, so its little canyons were briefly torrential. *A vivid case of ice-age drainage shaping a present-day road hazard.*
- **Glacial Lake Albany** — the proglacial lake (Poughkeepsie → Glens Falls, ~160 mi) that filled the Hudson Valley ~15,000 yrs ago and left the silt/clay behind. Its **delta and lake-bed deposits are the substrate of the whole clay-slide hazard** in the register. Its ~330-ft (near Albany) tilted shoreline ≈ a map of where the clay slides can happen. **Worth building as a map layer** (bedrock-overlay style).

## Deep-time bedrock POIs (Ordovician & Devonian)

- **Austin Glen Formation — New Baltimore Conservancy** (old Armstrong farm, County Rte 61, just S of New Baltimore village). ~465-Ma Ordovician **foreland-basin turbidites** (graywacke + black shale) shed off the rising ancestral Taconic Mts. An old shallow quarry shows **flute casts = petrified submarine landslides** (turbidity currents, 30–50 mph). The Conservancy has (or could have) a geology trail. *(Titus also built a geology trail at the Woodstock Land Conservancy's Sloan Gorge.)*
- **Normanskill Formation** — the dark graywacke + black shale that makes up much of the Hudson-Valley bedrock; Ordovician (~450 Ma) deep-sea (Normanskill Sea, ~tens of thousands of ft deep, ≈ today's Marianas). Deposited as submarine avalanches; "sole marks / flute casts" on slabs are petrified avalanche traces. Sample locality: an outcrop by the **Twin Bridges of the Northway** (rock brought in by Mrs. Deborah Teator of Greenville).
- **Cohoes Falls** (Cohoes) — the Mohawk tumbles over tilted **Normanskill Formation** sandstone (tilted by the Taconic Orogeny, ~450 Ma; resistant quartz sandstone makes the falls). Viewing stand off **Cataract Rd** (N off North Mohawk St). Cohoes is famed for three linked things: the **Cohoes mastodon** (skeleton at the NYS Museum), **Harmony Mills** (huge 19th-c. textile complex, built 1872, closed 1988, now upscale lofts — powered by the falls), and the falls themselves.
- **Catskill Front / Rte 23 W of the Rip Van Winkle Bridge** — towering **Devonian** (~400 Ma) outcrops of a shallow **tropical sea** ("NY was once like the Bahamas"). Same sea at **John Boyd Thacher State Park** (Helderberg escarpment — the register's karst country).
- **The Catskills = a petrified Devonian delta** (~385 Ma), holding **the world's oldest known fossil forest (Gilboa)** — once a tropical jungle. View the whole former Lake Albany basin from the hill at **Olana** (Frederic Church's estate, Greenport/Hudson).

## Landslide field-observation methods (Titus) — usable anywhere on valley clays

- **Leaning trees** flag an *active/incipient* slope: trees lean **back-uphill at the head** of a slump, **forward at the toe**.
- **Hemispherical "scoop" + abrupt headwall drop-off** at the top of a slope = the scar of a slide that *has already gone* (a past rotational slump).
- **Basements newly/chronically flooding** + reservoirs overtopping in ordinary years = rising water tables → slump precursor (the Titus 2005–07 "Yellow Alert" pattern).

## Landmark geology on Lake Albany deltas (at-risk context)

- **Hyde Park (Dutchess Co)** — built on the **Hyde Park Delta** (Crum Elbow Creek into Lake Albany). **FDR's Springwood** (low slump risk per Titus) and the **Vanderbilt Mansion "Hyde Park"** (ancient rotational-slide scars/headwalls on the foreset nearby) both sit on the delta's foreset edge. *Open question the Tituses can't resolve: are the ancient foreset slides one-time (post-drainage, ~15 ka) or ongoing?* — no way to tell yet.
- **Mount Ida, Troy** — the **Mt. Ida Delta** (Poesten Kill into Lake Albany); site of the 1837 and 1859 (St. Peters College) slides now in the register.
- **Gilboa Reservoir / Schoharie Reservoir** — the Tituses watch it overtop in wet years as a water-table barometer; the Gilboa fossil forest is nearby.

## Folklore / lost places / naming

- **"Murderer's Creek," Athens (Greene Co)** — a small stream entering the Hudson ~100 rods N of the upper settlement of Athens, named for the **1813 murder of Sally Hamilton** (daughter of Samuel Hamilton, Esq.). She left her sister's house ~8:30 pm on a Wednesday; stifled cries were heard near the creek; blood was found on the bridge planks; her body was found up the creek three days later. From an **1813 Hudson newspaper**, reprinted in **Barber & Howe, *Historical Collections of the State of New York* (1841)**, and again in the *Columbia Republican*, 15 Oct 1903 ("A Long Ago"). Classic Hudson-Valley true-crime folklore. *(A different "Murderer's Creek"/Moordener Kill exists near Coeymans — don't conflate.)*
- **White Sulphur Springs** — a lost/faded local place (POI lead from the OMOTM "best of times" article, earlier session). Worth pinning for the lore map.
- **Altamont naming / Lucy Cassidy** — village-naming lore lead (from the "last house in Altamont" memoir, earlier session).

## Register cross-links worth mapping

- **Bozenkill Rd corridor (Knox)** — the register's strongest multi-hazard node (1984/1990 tornadoes, 2010 Snowicane barn collapse, 2014 EF1, FEMA flood creek) — a natural lore-map "dot."
- **Palenville (foot of Kaaterskill Clove)** — two escarpment-front hazards stacked: 2006 Clove landslides (NY 23A) + the Pine Street avulsion-flood geohazard.
- **The Hudson rail line** — repeatedly severed by Lake Albany clay slides (Staatsburg 1903, etc.): a **Ways Atlas** node where a historical route keeps meeting the same geology.

## Appendix (2026-09-21, from the climate-register handoff's Folklore doc)

Lore only — kept out of the climate register by that project's own rule. Feeds the Hilltowns Lore Map, not the register. No coordinates asserted beyond the cave/site's own general location already in the Geology layer.

- **Knox Cave legends**: "Negley's Lost Room," a Nephite (Book of Mormon) tablet supposedly left in the cave, claimed "access to Lemuria," and the "Skeleton Passage." All folklore layered onto the same cave already pinned in Geology — none of this is asserted as fact.
- **Old Skull Cave**: a bone-chamber / ground-sloth legend (a supposed prehistoric-animal bone deposit) — unverified, associated with the Old Skull Cave (filled) / New Skull Cave pins already in Geology.
- **Clarksville Cave**: dated wall carvings from 1839 and 1864 reported inside — graffiti/visitor marks, not a geological or register-grade event, but a nice detail for a Clarksville Cave lore card if one is ever built.
- **McFail's death, folklorized as "the 1870s"**: the popular telling sometimes drifts the date later than the real one. The corrected date is **July 1, 1854** (Stone 2005, NCKMS paper — see the climate register's Sources sheet) — Roscoe's 1882 history had both the name and year slightly wrong, which is likely why the date has drifted in retellings since.
- **Barton Hill "hollow hill"**: a general local reputation for the hill being riddled with unmapped cave passage (consistent with the Caboose Cave / Single X Cave / Joober Hole cluster already flagged as coordinates-needed in Geology).
