About this atlas

Explore these hills…

A living atlas of the NY Helderberg-Hudson Hilltowns — folklore and legends gathered from historical sources, alongside the places, businesses, and happenings of Berne, Knox, Rensselaerville, and Westerlo. Every tale lists its original teller and source, and every pin is placed only as confidently as the record allows.
Additions, suggestions, corrections, complaints about the universe? Message us on Instagram at @NYHilltowners
